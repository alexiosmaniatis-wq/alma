## Maintenance & Support

### Version Support Policy

- **Current (0.x)**: Active development, frequent updates
- **Stable (1.x)**: Long-term support, critical fixes only
- **Deprecated (2.x)**: End of life, no support

### Release Cycle

- Patch releases (0.1.1): Bug fixes, bi-weekly
- Minor releases (0.2.0): New features, quarterly
- Major releases (1.0.0): Breaking changes, annually

### Support Timeline

- **0.1.0**: November 2026 - September 2027 (minor support)
- **0.2.0-0.9.0**: Ongoing development
- **1.0.0**: September 2027 - September 2032 (5-year LTS)

## Priorities

Development priorities are determined by:

1. **Community demand**: Most-requested features
2. **Use cases**: Utility to users (estimated impact)
3. **Complexity**: Resources required to implement
4. **Stability**: Risk and maintenance burden
